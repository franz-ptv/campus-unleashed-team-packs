# Campus Unleashed Hackathon: team packs

Everything your team needs for the hackathon. **Take only your own team's folder.**

1. The organisers tell you your **team number**. Open the folder `Team <number>`.
2. Download the files in it:
   - **Easiest:** open each file in your folder and click the **download button** (the arrow, "Download raw file").
   - **Or:** green **Code** button → **Download ZIP** (the whole repo), unzip it and keep only your folder.
3. What's inside:
   - `Project/Project Leaflet.pdf`: the project theme and how it's judged.
   - `Challenges/TW-XXXX.zip`: your hacking challenges. Unzip it and start with `README.txt`.
   - `Challenges/Hacking Challenges Leaflet.pdf`: rules, scoring, tools and how to hand in your flags.

Flags are different for every team. Another team's flags are worth **0 points** for you, so only use your own folder.

Questions? Ask an organiser. Good luck, and have fun!
